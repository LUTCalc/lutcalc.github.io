How It Works

Step 1: Select or Build

Choose a camera preset or input custom log parameters. Define input gamma, color space, and target.

Step 2: Shape the Look

Adjust exposure shifts, curves, saturation, and matrices. Visualize changes in real-time split-view.

Step 3: Analyze and Refine

Examine 3D LUT volumes or 1D curves. Validate against reference footage to perfect the transform.

Step 4: Export and Apply

Generate LUT files (.cube, .3dl) and preview on your clips. Deploy directly to post-production or camera.
